    feat(trip): add W9 transactional outbox for trip intents
    
    Persist every outgoing Trip obligation in the same EF unit of work as the business transition, then let C's shared relay claim/publish/mark it.
    
    - RideRequestedV1, CancelMatchingRoundV1 and ReleaseDriverReservationV1 are staged via TripOutboxIntents/TripOutboxWriter; Payment commands stay W10.
    - Deterministic messageIds make republish after an uncertain confirm or restart safe.
    - Create path is broker-free: a broker outage or crash after commit leaves a durable PENDING row.
    - EF migration mirrors the shared outbox_messages contract exactly; EF owns schema, relay never calls EnsureSchemaAsync.
    - Relay worker re-checks DB/broker readiness each cycle so outages do not burn attempts into FAILED_VISIBLE.
    - Add W09 integration proofs (atomicity, OCC, replay, crash/restart, lost mark, metrics) and intent unit tests.
